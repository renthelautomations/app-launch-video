# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, critique work rather than people, assume good intent, and help keep
this a welcoming space for people of every background and experience level. Harassment, discrimination and
personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository owner's
GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions
or ban contributors who violate these standards.
