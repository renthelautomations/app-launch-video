## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added
- `app-launch-video` skill: workflow, 12 production rules with reasons, phases from storyboard to delivery.
- References: visual direction, motion system (Apple spring presets, patterns, anti-patterns), sound design,
  QA kit, formats and delivery, review checklist and lessons log.
- Storyboard template at true 1920×1080 with a built-in QA engine (overlap, clipping, bounds, title-safe,
  minimum type size, hero centering).
- Remotion starter: single-source timeline, motion primitives, brand/component layer, 34 s example film,
  16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 compositions.
- Cue-driven soundtrack renderer: generated score, tuned SFX families, optional Kenney CC0 samples,
  binaural HRTF or pan fallback, ducking, licensed-track override.
- Scripts: logo tracing with optical-centering hint, HRTF and CC0 sample fetchers, multi-format delivery
  with two-pass loudness normalization, six QA scripts.
